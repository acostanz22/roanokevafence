Commercial Fencing in Roanoke, VA

A commercial fence closes a lot, a storage yard, or a job site, and the gates have to work for the traffic. We install chain link, ornamental aluminum or steel, and swing or sliding gates.

When the job is commercial

Use this page if the fence is for a business, a parking area, a yard, or a site that needs a gate a truck can clear. Ornamental metal fits a frontage that should stay open. Chain link fits security and storage.

Barbed wire or a razor top belongs on a commercial security spec only. We do not treat that hardware as a house option. Privacy screen is available on chain link when the lot needs to block a view.

How a commercial job is scoped

We price the use and the gates, then the footage.

  1. 1

    Start from the site

    Parking lot, storage yard, job site, or a frontage that should stay visible. The use picks the material.

  2. 2

    Match the spec

    Height, coating, privacy screen, and a barbed top only if the site calls for it. The estimate names each of those.

  3. 3

    Plan the openings

    People and trucks do not share one gate. A sliding gate is the usual answer when a swing leaf would block the drive.

  4. 4

    Walk it when it is up

    We check gaps, latches, and anything left in the way of traffic before we call the line finished.

What changes a commercial price

A commercial foot is not a backyard foot. Hardware and openings move the number.

Height and weight

Taller fence and heavier pipe cost more than a light residential chain-link line of the same length.

Gates

Swing, double drive, and sliding gates are different builds. The clear opening width is the number we need.

Screen or security top

Windscreen, privacy slats, and a barbed or razor top are add-ons with their own hardware. They are not assumed.

Access and schedule

A lot that has to stay partly open takes more coordination. That time belongs on the estimate, not after the crew arrives.

Commercial fence questions

What do you install on a commercial property?

Chain link, ornamental aluminum or steel, and gates. Privacy screen and windscreen go on chain link when the site needs to block a view. Barbed wire or a razor top is only for a commercial security spec, not for a house. The estimate names the material instead of calling all of it iron.

Can you fence a parking lot or a storage yard?

Yes. Those jobs are about the opening as much as the fence: where cars stack, where a truck turns, and which gate has to stay clear. A swing gate that blocks the drive is the wrong hardware. A sliding gate is the usual fix when the apron is short. We need that detail before we price the posts.

How is commercial chain link different from a backyard fence?

The commercial line is usually taller, with heavier pipe and fittings, and the gates take more traffic. A residential chain-link fence can be lighter because it is closing a yard, not a lot that trucks use. Using the house spec on a commercial yard is how a fence gets loose at the gates. We price the use, not just the footage.

Do you install driveway and sliding gates?

Yes. We install walk gates, driveway swing gates, and sliding gates. A sliding gate is the right hardware when a swing leaf would hit a parked car or block the lane. The opening width decides the post and the track, and that is a large part of the price. Measure the clear opening as best you can before the visit.

How should a property manager plan the work?

Tell us when the lot can be partly closed and which entrance has to stay open. The schedule depends on the job in front of yours, so we will not promise a standing day of the week on this site. We will say when the crew is coming and what has to be moved before we dig. A person who can answer questions on site matters as much as the material.

Can you match an ornamental look on a storefront?

Yes. A black ornamental fence is a common frontage when chain link would look like the back of the property. It does not hide the building. If you need a screen, that is privacy fence or chain link with a screen, and it is a different product. Say whether people should see through the line or whether the lot should be hidden.
