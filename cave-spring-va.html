Questions about fencing in Cave Spring

Why can a Cave Spring backyard cost more than a city lot?

Many yards here are deeper than the lots in central Roanoke, so the footage adds up. Price follows that length, then the gates, the slope, and whether an old fence has to come out. A long level line is still simpler than a short line full of roots. The footage has to be known before the number means anything.

Is vinyl a better fit than wood on a longer run?

On a long privacy line, stain and board repairs show up more often than they do on a short side yard. Vinyl is the request when you want that length without a yearly finish. Wood is the request when you want to choose the stain and you will maintain it. Neither one is automatic for every yard.

Can you mix a privacy fence and an open fence on one lot?

Yes. A common layout is privacy along the neighbor and picket, aluminum, or split rail where the lot opens up. Those are different materials and different posts, so they are separate lines on the estimate. Say where you want the look to change.

Can the crew get materials into a backyard that is already fenced?

Often, but it changes the labor. A side gate that is too narrow for panels means carrying pieces through. If a truck can reach the rear line, the job moves faster. Tell us which one you have so the estimate matches the access.
