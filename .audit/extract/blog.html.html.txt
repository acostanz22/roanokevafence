The Fence Installation Process by a Professional Contractor Proper Fence Installation: A Professional Contractor's Approach 
Installing a fence is not an easy task, and that's why it's a job best left to professional contractors. The fence installation process by a professional contractor involves several crucial steps that ensure the fence is correctly installed and functions optimally . 
Step 1: Inspection and Planning 
The first step in a professional fence installation involves inspecting the site. This inspection helps the contractor determine key factors such as the type of soil, obstructions, and terrain. Then, based on the inspection, the contractor will plan and design the fence, taking into consideration the client's specific preferences and needs. 
Step 2: Setting the Posts 
The second step involves setting the fence posts. The posts are the foundation of the fence, and their placement and installation need to be accurate. The professional contractor will use high-quality materials and tools to ensure the fence posts are secure and level. 
Step 3: Fence Installation 
After the fence posts are installed, the professional contractor will proceed with the actual fence installation. The fence panels are then attached to the posts with screws and other fasteners to create a solid fence structure that can withstand harsh weather conditions and other potential damage. 
In addition to these steps, professional contractors also provide regular maintenance and repair services for the fence to ensure it remains in good condition and lasts for many years. Installation and FinishingThe process of setting up and completing a fence is a crucial task that requires careful attention to detail. To make sure the installation and finishing meet expectations, one must follow these three essential steps. 
Ground Preparation: Before starting the installation, it's vital to do some groundwork. Surveying the area will help determine the fence's position and ensure there are no underground utilities or obstructions. Next, mark the fence outline using stakes and string, ensuring it follows the property line. 
Installation: Once you've prepared the ground, start installing fence posts at every corner first, then adjust accordingly to complete the line post spacing. The posts should be set into holes dug in cement for stability. Once the posts are stable, attach rails and pickets, ensuring alignment as per instruction manual. 
Finishing: After installation, apply wood sealer or paint to protect the fence from weather damages such as dampness and sunlight exposure. Final adjustments such as adding gate hardware will also be made. Regular Cleaning and Inspection Regular Maintenance and Inspection of Your Fence

Tighten any loose screws or nails on the framework or pickets
