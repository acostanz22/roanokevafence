Vinyl Fencing Despite being more expensive than wood, vinyl fencing (also known as PVC and/or plastic fencing) is still preferred for two main reasons. Maintenance is the major factor. After installation, painting or staining is not necessary. You won't notice any sagging or warping. The majority of what you see today will still be there in ten years. Customers appreciate the fact that there is neither a "ugly" nor "beautiful" side to the fence. A vinyl fence allows two neighbors to share its benefits without having to determine who gets the flat side and who gets the rails. 

While vinyl fencing initially costs more than wood, once you stain a wood fence, you typically have a similar price in both fences. ​
