What fence work looks like in Salem

House lots in Salem are often a similar size to Roanoke neighborhoods, so the common job is a backyard privacy fence and a walk gate to the side yard. Front lines, when someone wants one, are picket or aluminum so the house stays visible.

Salem writes its own rules. A permit answer from the city of Roanoke does not automatically cover a Salem parcel. Name Salem on the estimate and check with the city before posts go in.

Slope, gates, and commercial lots

The ground still rolls. A fence that looks level in a photo has to step or rack when the yard falls away from the house. We measure that on site because it changes posts, cuts, and whether a gate will swing.

Shops, churches, and small commercial lots are a different request from the backyard. Those lines are chain link or an open metal fence, with a gate sized for the cars that actually use the drive. Tell us if a truck has to enter.

Questions about fencing in Salem

Do you install fences in Salem, or only inside Roanoke?

Salem is part of the regular service area. The crew, the materials, and the way we estimate are the same. What changes is the lot, and which office you ask about a permit. Salem is its own city, so a Roanoke city answer does not automatically cover a Salem parcel.

What do Salem homeowners usually ask for?

The usual house job is a backyard privacy fence in wood or vinyl, with a walk gate to the side yard. Front lines, when someone wants one, are more often picket or aluminum so the house stays visible. Businesses more often need chain link or an open metal fence than a solid wall along the front.

Can you price a fence that steps down a Salem yard?

Yes. A catalog panel built for flat ground will gap or bind on a yard that falls away from the house. We measure the drop on site. The slope is part of the price because it changes posts, panel cuts, and how a gate swings.

Can you fence a Salem business as well as a house?

Yes. Shops and small lots are commercial jobs: chain link, ornamental metal, and gates sized for the traffic. A driveway gate is different hardware from a walk gate. Tell us if trucks need to enter so that opening is on the estimate from the start.
