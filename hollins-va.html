What fence work looks like in Hollins

Larger parcels are where split rail, horse fence, and field fence belong. A horse needs a rail it can see. Cattle are a wire or electric question. Wrapping those acres in privacy panels is usually a waste of the budget.

House lots are different. They still ask for wood or vinyl along the shared side and something lower toward the road so the house stays visible. Hollins is in Roanoke County, so city fence rules are the wrong office to call.

Grade toward the ridges

The ground rises as you leave the valley floor. Open aluminum and rail fence can often follow that grade. Tall privacy panels more often step, because a racked privacy panel opens a gap at the bottom.

A farm gate has to clear a truck or a trailer. Tell us the width before we price hinge posts. A light line post will not carry that leaf, and the gate is a large part of a Hollins pasture job.

Questions about fencing in Hollins

Do you build horse and farm fence in Hollins?

Yes. Larger parcels north of the city are where horse fence, split rail, and cattle fence make more sense than privacy panels around the whole boundary. A horse needs a fence it can see. Cattle are more often held with barbed wire or electric fence. We need to know which animals are there before we price the line.

Should a larger Hollins lot be privacy fence all the way around?

Usually no. Privacy fence by the foot gets expensive on a long boundary, and it is often only needed beside a neighbor or around a patio. The rest of the line can be rail or field fence. We would rather price the screen where you want a screen.

Can the fence follow a slope instead of stepping every panel?

On open aluminum and on rail fence, the line can often follow the grade. Tall privacy panels more often step, because racking them opens a gap at the bottom. Hollins ground rises out of the valley, so this shows up on the walk-through. The method is part of the look and part of the price.

Do house lots in Hollins still get wood and vinyl?

Yes. A house lot can want vinyl or wood along the neighbor and a lower fence toward the road so the house stays visible. That is a different job from fencing a pasture. Tell us which part of the property you want closed in.
