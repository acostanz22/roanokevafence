What fence work looks like in Vinton

The typical call is a wood privacy fence that has lost boards, leaned, or rotted at the ground. If the posts are still solid, we can replace boards and a gate. If the posts are soft, new boards will follow the old lean.

On a narrow lot the fence is close to the neighbor. Wood has a frame side and a finished side. Many vinyl privacy panels look the same from both yards, which is why vinyl comes up when the line is shared.

Small yards and the property line

Questions about fencing in Vinton

Can you replace a wood fence in Vinton without replacing every post?

Sometimes. If the posts are still solid in the ground, boards and a gate can be replaced on their own. If the posts are soft, twisted, or leaning, new boards on those posts will follow the lean. We check the posts before we tell you it is a repair.

Which side of a privacy fence faces the neighbor?

A wood privacy fence has a frame side and a finished side, so say which way you want the finished face. Many vinyl privacy panels look the same from both yards, which matters when the fence sits close to the neighbor. We do not decide the property line. Confirm it before we dig.

Do smaller Vinton yards change the price?

A shorter line can cost less in material and still take time at the gates, because the gate is a larger share of a small yard. Access matters too. If materials have to come through a tight side gap, the job is slower than a line the truck can reach.

Is vinyl worth it when I am replacing old wood?

It is the usual alternative when you do not want to stain the replacement. Vinyl usually costs more to put in than a basic wood privacy fence. The tradeoff is the upkeep you are trying to stop. If you want stained wood and you will maintain it, wood is still a sound choice.
