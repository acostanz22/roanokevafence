Match the fence to the job

Use this to pick a page. The price still depends on the line at the property.

Vinyl Fence

Choose vinyl for a privacy or picket fence you do not want to stain. A typical privacy panel looks finished from both sides, which helps on a shared lot line.

Wood Fence

Choose wood when you want privacy, picket, or dog-ear boards you can stain. Plan on upkeep, especially where the boards meet the soil.

Chain-link Fence

Choose chain link to enclose a yard, a dog, or a work lot without paying for a solid screen. Gates and the coating change the price more than the footage slogan does.

Aluminum Fence

Choose aluminum when the fence should stay open, including around a pool. Sections can follow a slope more easily than a tall privacy panel.

Farm Fence

Choose farm fence for horses, cattle, or a long property edge. The animal decides between rail, pipe and cable, wire, or electric.

Commercial Fencing

Choose a commercial build for a lot, a yard, or a gate that trucks use. The pipe, the height, and the hardware are specified apart from a backyard fence.
