Fence Service Areas

We install fences in Roanoke and the towns around it. The pages below are about the fence work in each place, not a town history.

Roanoke, VA

City lots are tighter, and the usual request is privacy between houses. Hillsides and corner lots change the posts and the gate more than the neighborhood name does.

Fence details

Salem, VA

Salem is its own city on the west side of Roanoke. House lots usually want backyard privacy fence, and businesses more often want chain link or aluminum.

Fence details

Vinton, VA

Much of the Vinton work is a wood privacy fence that has leaned or rotted. Narrow lots make the finished side and the gate location part of the plan.

Fence details

Cave Spring, VA

Cave Spring backyards are often longer than city lots, so footage drives the price. Vinyl is a frequent request on those longer privacy lines.

Fence details

Hollins, VA

Hollins is where farm and horse fence fits larger parcels north of the city. House lots still use wood or vinyl along the neighbor.

Fence details

Roanoke County, VA

The county mixes subdivision privacy fences and pasture lines. County fence rules are not the same as Roanoke city rules.

Fence details

Neighborhoods are not separate fence types

A steep street in southwest Roanoke and a steep street in Salem ask for the same thing on install day: posts that follow the grade, and a gate that still swings. Naming every neighborhood would not change the material or the price method.

What does change is the lot and the locality. City yards are tighter. Cave Spring backyards run longer. Hollins and other county parcels pick up farm fence. Salem and Vinton are not the City of Roanoke for permit questions. Start with the place page, then the service that matches the job.
