About Roanoke VA Fence Company

We install and repair residential, commercial, and farm fences in Roanoke and the nearby towns. The work happens at the property. This site does not list a showroom address.

Request an estimate Call (540) 234-2867

What we install

Vinyl, wood, chain link, aluminum, farm fence, and commercial fencing and gates. If you need a horse fence, a pool fence, or a wood privacy fence replaced, start with the matching service page or call (540) 234-2867.

An estimate starts with the line: approximate length, material, gates, slope, and access. We do not publish a price per foot, because a flat backyard and a rocky hillside are not the same job.

How an estimate works

Call or use the form. Estimates are free. Say whether a fence is already there and whether you want it repaired or taken out. If the posts are the problem, we will say so instead of selling boards onto a bad line.

We cover Roanoke, Salem, Vinton, Cave Spring, Hollins, and Roanoke County. If the property is farther out, say so on the form and we will tell you whether we can take the trip.

This page does not list a year the company opened, a license number, or a warranty term. Those details are not published here. What we will do is price the fence in front of us and build that fence.
